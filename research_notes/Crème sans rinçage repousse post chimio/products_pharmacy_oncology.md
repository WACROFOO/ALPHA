# Leave-in hair creams from French pharmacy and oncology brands for short post-chemo regrowth: product candidates and INCI analysis

Research date: 8 November 2026. Prices are those shown on the cited retailer or brand pages on that date. Promotions are noted. INCI lists are quoted from the cited page. Several retailers (INCI Beauty, Notino, Pharmacie Lafayette, Pharmao, Oncovia) blocked automated access, so some lists come from secondary retailers. Each source is named.

Abbreviations: "Parfum, no declared allergens" means the label shows PARFUM/FRAGRANCE but no individually named EU fragrance allergens. See Inferences.

---

## Q1. Do oncology-specific brands (MÊME, Ozalys/Oncovia, others) make a leave-in styling cream for regrowth hair?

### Takeaway
No. I found no oncology-specific French brand that sells a leave-in styling or anti-frizz cream for the hair lengths. MÊME's only leave-in hair product is a scalp mist, La Brume pour le cuir chevelu. It was tested on 70 women undergoing treatment and is a good scalp complement, but it does not style. MÊME's mask and Ozalys's scalp milk are both rinse-off. So the styling cream has to come from mainstream pharmacy brands.

### Cited Findings
- MÊME's "Alopécie, repousse des cheveux" page lists 11 products. The hair ones are the shampoo-soin (19,50 €), the Masque réparateur (25,00 €), the Brume pour le cuir chevelu (26,00 €), and kits. No leave-in cream for the lengths is listed. — [MÊME, catégorie alopécie/repousse](https://www.memecosmetics.fr/fr/38-alopecie-repousse-des-cheveux-)
- **MÊME Masque réparateur pour les cheveux (200 ml, 25 €) is rinse-off.** Directions: leave on 1 minute, then rinse. Alternatively a 5-minute mask, or overnight once a month, always rinsed. The brand says it is "formulé pour être adapté aux personnes sous traitements anticancéreux". It was tested on 20 volunteers with sensitive or atopic scalps. The brand says it is free of sulfates, parabens and silicones. — [MÊME Masque réparateur](https://www.memecosmetics.fr/fr/cuir-chevelu/495-masque-reparateur-cheveux.html)

- AFSOS's patient guide on alopecia advises applying a pharmaceutical-grade leave-in hair lotion on wet hair to hydrate and soften the scalp. This is from the search summary; I did not read the PDF itself. — [AFSOS, "La chute / alopécie" (PDF)](https://afsos.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/LA-CHUTE-ALOPECIE.pdf)
- Regrowth hair can come back "plus fins, bouclés, plus secs". Hospital sources give regrowth starting about 2–3 months after the last treatment, at about 1 cm per month. They advise mild shampoo, washing less often and air-drying. — [CHUV](https://www.chuv.ch/fr/offre-en-soins/maladies-et-parcours-de-soins/conseils-de-soin/conseil-de-soin/chuvbricks_conseilssoins_4/effets-de-la-chimiotherapie-sur-les-cheveux-et-la-pilosite); [Clinique des Cèdres Toulouse (PDF)](https://clinique-cedres-toulouse.ramsaysante.fr/sites/default/files/biblio/domaines/clinique_des_cedres/SO-D-290.pdf); [Institut national du cancer, guide chute des cheveux](https://www.cancer.fr/Media/files/guide-traitements-du-cancer-et-chute-des-cheveux)

### Inferences
- The MÊME Brume is the only leave-in product I found with use-testing on people undergoing cancer treatment. It suits a sensitive or dry post-chemo scalp but will not tame or shape the hair. It could be paired with a light pharmacy cream on the lengths.
- The MÊME mask could be useful once a week (rinsed). It is not a substitute for the requested leave-in.

### Gaps
- I did not find any leave-in hair product from Les Laboratoires de Biarritz (Alga Maris), Codexial, Evaux or Lierac. These were searched only briefly, so absence is likely but not certain.
- Oncovia's full catalogue was inaccessible (403).
- I found no list of socio-esthéticienne recommendations naming leave-in creams (Rose Magazine, Ligue contre le cancer, CEW).
- The full verbatim INCI of the Ozalys Lait Doux was not retrieved.

## Q2. Which pharmacy/parapharmacy leave-in creams are candidates, and what exactly is in them?

### Takeaway
I checked 13 pharmacy leave-ins and recorded the full INCI for 12; Typology's could not be retrieved. Only two light smoothing creams avoid named allergens on the label, though both contain parfum: **Klorane Nutrition Crème nourrissante sans rinçage (Mangue)** and **Phyto 7 Élixir Crème de jour universelle**. A third is gentle and low-fragrance-allergen for a sensitive scalp: **Klorane Apaisant Après-shampoing à la Pivoine**, which the brand says can be used "avec ou sans rinçage". Every styling leave-in found contains parfum. The only fragrance-free leave-ins found are scalp products (Ducray Sensinol Spray SOS), not styling creams.

### Cited Findings

## Q3. Which products suit the goal (short 3–7 cm frizzy regrowth, tamed toward waves rather than curls, sensitive scalp)?

### Takeaway
To get waves rather than curls, choose a light **smoothing** cream (Klorane Nutrition Mangue, Phyto 7 Élixir, Natessance Ricin) used in pea-sized amounts on towel-dried hair and combed or brushed through. Avoid **curl-defining** creams that are scrunched in: Natessance Karité, Lazartigue Curl Specialist, and curl ranges such as René Furterer Sublime Curl.

### Cited Findings
- Klorane Nutrition is "fluide, légère… sans alourdir… fini satiné", and is used "avant le brushing ou en finition". — [Klorane](https://www.klorane.com/fr-fr/p/nutrition-creme-nourrissante-sans-rincage-3282771001142-14c39aab)
- Phyto 7 Élixir "Lisse et discipline… anti-frisottis 48h… sans alourdir", for all dry hair including curly. — [Redcare](https://www.redcare-pharmacie.fr/beaute/BE04944195/phyto-paris-phyto-7-elixir-creme-de-jour-universelle.htm)
- Natessance Ricin: "texture fluide" that smooths "sans alourdir". — [La Santé.net](https://lasante.net/espace-para/beaute-et-soins/beaute-et-soins-cheveux/natessance-creme-de-soin-capillaire-sans-rincage-100-ml.html)
- Klorane Pivoine: "gelée-baume" texture, with or without rinsing, for a sensitive scalp. — [Klorane](https://www.klorane.com/fr-fr/p/apres-shampoing-a-la-pivoine-bio-3282770145700-14c39aab)
- Phyto Lait démêlant: "texture légère, n'alourdit pas", "disciplinés sans frisottis". — [Phyto](https://fr.phyto.com/p/ph1007031aa-douceur-lait-demelant-express-sans-rincage-150ml)
- Curl-defining: Natessance Karité ("définir les boucles", press the curls from ends to roots) — [Soin et Nature](https://www.soin-et-nature.com/fr/27088-natessance-karite-creme-sans-rincage-100-ml.html). Lazartigue Curl Specialist ("définit instantanément les boucles", scrunch, diffuser) — [Pharmacie des Drakkars](https://www.pharmaciedesdrakkars.com/lazartigue-curl-specialist-creme-disciplinante).
- Rich or heavy formulas: René Furterer Karité Nutri (shea oil, castor oil, shea butter high in the list) — [Je vais mieux merci](https://www.jevaismieuxmerci.com/karite-nutri-creme-de-jour-nutrition-intense-100-ml-21151.html). ### Inferences
- On 3–7 cm hair, a tiny amount (half a hazelnut) applied to towel-dried hair and smoothed with a brush or fingers, ideally with gentle air or blow-drying while brushing, should loosen curls toward waves. Scrunching and diffusing do the opposite.
- The two lightest options for a reactive scalp are Klorane Pivoine (very light, may under-tame strong frizz) and Phyto 7 Élixir or Klorane Mangue (more smoothing power). Possible routine: MÊME Brume or Ducray Sensinol Spray SOS on the scalp, and Klorane Mangue or Phyto 7 Élixir on the hair.

### Gaps
- No product page or review explicitly addresses 3–7 cm post-chemo hair. Texture fit is inferred from brand descriptions.
