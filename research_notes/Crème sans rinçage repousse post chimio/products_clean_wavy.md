# Leave-in creams/conditioners from clean, organic, fragrance-free and wavy-specialist brands (France/EU): INCI analysis for short post-chemo regrowth

Scope: wavy/curly specialist brands and clean/organic/fragrance-free brands only. French pharmacy brands (Ducray, Klorane, La Rosée, Avène, Bioderma, Uriage, Phyto, René Furterer, Typology, Mustela) and MÊME are covered by another researcher and are left out here.

Method note: INCIDecoder (incidecoder.com, which now 301-redirects to inkeedecoder.com) pages were downloaded and the full ingredient blocks extracted word for word. Where possible, lists were cross-checked against the brand site or an EU retailer page. Prices and stock were read from retailer pages on 2026-11-08. Stock changes often.

## Q1. Innersense Organic Beauty: Fragrance Free Sweet Spirit Leave-in Conditioner

### Takeaway
This is the lightest genuinely unscented option found: a sprayable milk with aloe, glycerin and tamanu oil, preserved with sodium benzoate, potassium sorbate and benzyl alcohol. It has no parfum, no essential oils, no phenoxyethanol, no parabens, no PEGs and no silicones. It is well suited to 3–7 cm hair that should be smoothed rather than curled. One caveat: one database lists a fragrance allergen that the brand/retailer lists omit, so check the box.

### Cited Findings
- **Product / size / price / where:** "Innersense Sweet Spirit Leave in Conditioner – Fragrance Free (295ml)", £26.00 on Cult Beauty UK. Cult Beauty also has a .com EUR storefront and lists a 59 ml travel size. — [Cult Beauty UK](https://www.cultbeauty.co.uk/p/innersense-fragrance-free-sweet-spirit-leave-in-conditioner-295ml/17331193/); [Cult Beauty (EUR site)](https://www.cultbeauty.com/p/innersense-fragrance-free-sweet-spirit-leave-in-conditioner-295ml/17331193/)
- The 59 ml fragrance-free size is also listed on Face the Future's EU storefront. — [Face the Future EU](https://eu.facethefuture.store/products/innersense-sweet-spirit-leave-in-conditioner-fragrance-free-59ml)
- **Full INCI (brand-retailer version, Detox Market; the same opening appears on Cult Beauty):** "Water, Aloe Barbadensis Leaf Juice\*, Behentrimonium Chloride, Glycerin, Caprylic/Capric, Triglyceride, Cetearyl Alcohol, Gluconolactone, Sodium Benzoate, Potassium Sorbate, Hydrolyzed Quinoa, Calophyllum Inophyllum Seed Oil\*, Citric Acid, Aspalathus Linearis Leaf Extract\*, Chamomilla Recutita (Matricaria) Flower Extract\*, Eucalyptus Globulus Leaf Extract\*, Ginkgo Biloba Leaf Extract\*, Honey Extract\*, Dipropylene Glycol, Calcium Gluconate, Benzyl Alcohol. \*Certified Organic" — [The Detox Market](https://www.thedetoxmarket.com/products/innersense-fragrance-free-sweet-spirit-leave-in-conditioner); [Cult Beauty UK](https://www.cultbeauty.co.uk/p/innersense-fragrance-free-sweet-spirit-leave-in-conditioner-295ml/17331193/)

Retailers say it is made without essential oils, and Grove says it is free of parabens, PEGs, sulfates, phthalates and synthetic fragrance. — [Grove](https://www.grove.co/products/fragrance-free-sweet-spirit-leave-in-conditioner)

Do not confuse the two. ### Inferences
- Checklist: no parfum or essential oils (per the brand/retailer INCI). No MIT/MCI, formaldehyde releasers, parabens, phenoxyethanol, D4/D5/D6, BHT/BHA, PEGs, alcohol denat., benzophenones or mineral oil. Benzyl alcohol is the preservative. Dipropylene glycol is a solvent, most likely the carrier for the extracts.
- Botanical *extracts* (chamomile, eucalyptus leaf, rooibos, ginkgo, honey) are not essential oils, but chamomile can cross-react in people allergic to Asteraceae plants. A patch test behind the ear is sensible.
- Helpful: aloe, glycerin, behentrimonium chloride (detangling/anti-frizz), cetearyl alcohol, a light triglyceride and tamanu oil, hydrolyzed quinoa.
- Texture: the lightest product in this list, with very low hold. It smooths and detangles rather than defining curls, which makes it a good fit for steering 3–7 cm regrowth toward waves. It can be layered under a little cream if more control is needed.
- Verdict: **Recommended**, provided the box shows no "Amyl Cinnamal".

## Q3. Curlsmith: Fragrance-Free Leave-In (formerly "Shine Cream")

### Takeaway
This is the fragrance-free version of Curlsmith's popular Weightless Air Dry Cream. The brand recommends it for wavy hair (2A–2C), it is a light cream that "smooths cuticles", and it is easy to buy in the EU. INCI caveats: castor oil is the second ingredient (can feel tacky), it contains phenoxyethanol, and it contains **hops (Humulus lupulus) extract**, the source of the potent phytoestrogen 8-prenylnaringenin. That is a precautionary flag if her cancer was hormone-sensitive. It also has jasmine and lemon-peel extracts, and one reviewer said the "fragrance-free" label was misleading.

### Cited Findings
- **Full INCI (INCIDecoder):** "Water (Aqua), Ricinus Communis (Castor) Seed Oil, Dicaprylyl Carbonate, Cetearyl Alcohol, Orbignya Speciosa (Babassu) Kernel Oil, Behentrimonium Chloride, Panthenol, Astrocaryum Murumuru Seed Butter, Simmondsia Chinensis (Jojoba) Seed Oil, Butyrospermum Parkii (Shea) Butter, Humulus Lupulus (Hops) Extract, Jasminum Officinale (Jasmine) Flower Extract, Citrus Limon (Lemon) Peel Extract, Polyquaternium-10, Guar Hydroxypropyltrimonium Chloride, Tocopherol, Disodium EDTA, Ethylhexylglycerin, Glycerin, Citric Acid, Sodium Hydroxide, Potassium Sorbate, Sodium Benzoate, Phenoxyethanol" — [INCIDecoder](https://incidecoder.com/products/curlsmith-fragrance-free-leave-in). The brand page opens the same way ("WATER (AQUA/EAU), RICINUS COMMUNIS (CASTOR) SEED OIL, DICAPRYLYL CARBONATE, CETEARYL ALCOHOL…") and names hops, jasmine and lemon peel extracts. — [Curlsmith](https://curlsmith.com/products/fragrance-free-leave-in)
- **Claims/use:** "light leave-in moisturizing cream" for "All Curl Types with Fragrance Sensitivity" (Wavy 2A–2C, Curly, Coily). It is based on the Weightless Air Dry Cream, "hydrates and smooths cuticles without weighing hair down", and is dermatologist tested, 8 oz / 237 ml. Use a dime-sized amount on soaking-wet hair and smooth with "praying hands". — [Curlsmith](https://curlsmith.com/products/fragrance-free-leave-in)
- **EU availability/price:** Zalando DE €24.95 for 237 ml (listing showed out of stock); Sephora UK £23.00; also saricurls.de, lockenbox.com, parfumdreams. — [Zalando DE](https://en.zalando.de/curlsmith-fragrance-free-leave-in-conditioner-0-cuc34h00r-s11.html); [Sephora UK](https://www.sephora.co.uk/p/curlsmith-fragrance-free-leave-in); [saricurls.de](https://saricurls.de/en/products/curlsmith-shine-cream); [Lockenbox](https://www.lockenbox.com/en/products/curlsmith-fragrance-free-leave-in-237-ml); [Parfumdreams](https://www.parfumdreams.ie/Curlsmith/Hair-care/Conditioner-Leave-in/Fragrance-Free-Leave-In/index_143206.aspx)
- **Review caveat:** A Sephora UK reviewer said calling it "fragrance-free" is misleading and that people who need truly fragrance-free products should avoid it (seen in a search snippet; the page did not load directly). — [Sephora UK](https://www.sephora.co.uk/p/curlsmith-fragrance-free-leave-in)
- **Brand-commissioned claim:** In a brand study, 89% said it didn't weigh their hair down. This is marketing data. — [Curlsmith](https://curlsmith.com/products/fragrance-free-leave-in)
- **Hops phytoestrogen:** 8-prenylnaringenin from hops is often described as the most estrogenic phytoestrogen known, and it stimulated growth of estrogen-dependent MCF-7 breast cancer cells in vitro. Overall evidence is mixed and largely preclinical, and no data on topical cosmetic exposure were found. — [Wikipedia – 8-Prenylnaringenin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/8-Prenylnaringenin)

### Inferences
- Checklist: no parfum and no declared allergens. No MIT/MCI, formaldehyde releasers, parabens, silicones, PEGs, BHT or mineral oil. It does contain phenoxyethanol (with ethylhexylglycerin) and disodium EDTA. Lemon-peel and jasmine extracts are not essential oils, but they may give a faint scent. Hops extract is near the middle-end of the list, so the dose is probably low, but given a possibly hormone-sensitive cancer it is worth flagging and leaving to her oncologist or pharmacist.
- Helpful: behentrimonium chloride, panthenol, polyquaternium-10 and guar (anti-frizz film), cetearyl alcohol, light carbonate ester, glycerin.
- Weight: light-medium. Castor oil plus butters can be too much on very fine hair, so a pea-sized amount is plenty for 3–7 cm. Smoothing with "praying hands" pushes toward waves rather than ringlets.
- Verdict: **Recommended with caveats** (hops flag). If the cancer was ER+, prefer #1 or #2.

### Gaps
- Not found on Sephora France or Nocibé.
- No INCI Beauty or Yuka score found.
- No independent reviews on wavy hair comparing it to the scented original.

## Q11. Fit with the specific need: short (3–7 cm) post-chemo regrowth, wavy (not curly) goal, sensitive scalp, possibly hormone-sensitive cancer

### Takeaway
For short, frizzy regrowth that should be steered toward waves, light, low-hold smoothing leave-ins are the best fit: Innersense FF spray, Briogeo BGBK spray, and Curlsmith FF or SEEN creams. Richer "curl creams" (Bouclème FF, Only Curls FF) and gels define curls. Fragrance-free formulas are preferred for a reactive scalp. Hops extract (Curlsmith FF) is the only ingredient in the recommended set with a specific hormonal question attached.

### Cited Findings
- "Chemo curls": hair often grows back curlier after chemotherapy, often temporarily. Leave-in conditioners are commonly advised for dry, brittle regrowth. — [Anaono](https://anaono.com/blogs/dressing-room/chemo-curls-101-what-they-are-how-to-care-for-them); [John Frieda](https://johnfrieda.com/en-uk/blog/hair-care/hair-growth-after-cancer-treatment)

- Curlsmith markets its Fragrance-Free Leave-In as useful for fragrance-sensitive people (chemo-curl searches surfaced it). — [Curlsmith](https://curlsmith.com/products/fragrance-free-leave-in)
- Hops' 8-prenylnaringenin is a potent phytoestrogen; in vitro it stimulates MCF-7 (ER+) breast cancer cells. No topical-cosmetic exposure data were found. — [Wikipedia – 8-Prenylnaringenin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/8-Prenylnaringenin)

### Inferences
- **Ranking for this user (preliminary):**
  1. Innersense FF Sweet Spirit (lightest, unscented, no phenoxyethanol, no PEG; check the box for "Amyl Cinnamal").
  2. Briogeo Be Gentle Be Kind Detangling Spray (unscented, hypoallergenic claim, EU price €23).
  3. Curlsmith Fragrance-Free Leave-In (built for waves and easy to get in the EU; hops flag).
  4. SEEN FF Leave-In (excellent dermatologist-led formula; import only).
  5. Bouclème FF Curl Cream (cleanest curl cream; rich, defines curls; Paris stockist).
